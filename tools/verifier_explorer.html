About this tool

The Temporal Graph Verifier Explorer is a diagnostic instrument developed as part of an MSci dissertation at the University of St Andrews. It allows per-task inspection of structured LLM outputs verified against a temporal graph constraint library and a graph-grounded LTL fragment.

Each task record encodes the model's predicted events, typed temporal relations, step-indexed reasoning trace, and verification verdicts across seven axes: parse robustness, structural validity, trace grounding, representation fidelity, closure ordering, ambiguity discipline, and contradiction detection.
